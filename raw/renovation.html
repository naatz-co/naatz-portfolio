Work/Loft Renovation Hub

Renovation Hub

Manage all renovation data in one place.

In 2020, I led the redesign of the Renovation Hub, a back-office platform created to centralize renovation data and improve cost control. This tool enabled teams to track budgets and expenses in one place, creating a single source of truth.

Role & Services

Product Design

Year

2020

Context

Pandemic changed relationship with home and stimulated reforms. With the restrictive measures, people started to work and study remotely, and invest in their living environment. Due to the pandemic, the consumption of renovations and reforms in Brazil increased by 30% to about 40%.

The sector's revenue growth was the second-highest during the pandemic, with an increase of 5.5%.

According to Grupo Consumoteca, 55% of class A and 39% of class C made some decoration changes during this period.

Constraints

As the number of renovation projects increased, we realized the importance of having better cost control over them. To help our operation and teams, we created a back-office platform to inform our renovation budget and cost per apartment. Enabling RaaS teams to scale their operation based on well-established tools and processes from renovation design to planning

The Problem

Mybox was a back-office platform to help our operation and renovation squads gather data from apartment renovations. However, the platform was slow and confusing and didn't contain all the necessary information for the teams to use. Along with Mybox, we had other sources of data regarding renovation budget costs, but we lacked a single source of truth.

Business Problem

Low data accuracy generates less liquidity for apartments available for sale.

User Problem

The user is afraid of making bad decisions with the information they have.

The Challenge

Create a platform that centralizes renovation data costs in a single place.

Problems found in Mybox
the former renovation platform

Unused Information: Some information saved in Mybox isn’t being used, which adds unnecessary clutter.

Confusing Data: Users found some information that seemed unclear or contradictory.

Slow Performance: The platform runs slowly, frustrating for teams who use it often.

No Central Budget Tracking: The platform has no place to see all budget info, making it hard to keep track.

Different Terms: Some terms on the platform don’t match the terms teams use daily, leading to misunderstandings.

Facilitation (crazy eight)

We also did facilitation to consolidate ideas, determine which points we should focus on, and gather insights from different users.

Most voted features

As the platform is used at different times by teams related to renovations, it was essential to understand the most critical points for them when redesigning this tool.

With the crazy eight facilitation, I ranked the features that most appeared in the users' drawings to better understand what features we should strike first and estimate the time these changes would take.

Ideate + Iterate

With my team's help, I categorized the information needed in this new version.

Sketches

V1 - Layout improvements

The first version I made showed the apartment's construction status, contract name, description next to the closing dates, type of contractor, and prices. If users like to see the contract description, they could expand the content by clicking the chevron on the right side of the row.

V2 - Refining the idea

The second version had visual changes and improvements in the information covered, discarding data we discovered that wasn't important enough to be on this screen.

V3 - Input the best terms

In this updated version, we have simplified the language used to describe the features, making it easier to understand terms such as cost value, taxes, and contract margins. We have also made some visual changes, improving the interaction with filters and making the page more objective.

Final Concept & Results

Sucessful launch

The renovation budget features had a usability success rate of 91%.

Hub of information

We consolidated budget information on a single platform.

More productivity

New features improved the team’s workflow without using outside sources to obtain info.

Improved XP

Information is now reliable, clear, and the platform is easy to use.
