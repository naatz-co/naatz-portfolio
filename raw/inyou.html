InYou

Change your habits based on your DNA

Led the product design, branding, and visual identity for InYou, a habit tracker app that combines DNA analysis and guidance from a nutritionist to help users build healthier habits.

Role & Services

Product Design

Year

2020

InYou Health

InYou is a custom program that combines DNA analysis with lifestyle habits to help people achieve their health goals.

Overview

InYou is an app that provides personalized habit change programs based on habit analysis, diet, fitness routine, meditation, and DNA.

The Challenge

Create a user-friendly app that helps users build new daily habits through a simple interface.

The Users

An individual who purchased the health program from a nutritionist or directly with InYou.

User Journey

Mapping the journey gave me a better understanding of the user steps inside and outside the application before acquiring their genetic result and starting the program. It was crucial in measuring expectations and making the application as objective as possible.

Job Stories

I also created job stories to understand the situation of users and nutritionists

Nutritionist

I do not have a platform to keep up with my patient's daily lives the way I would like to. Knowing about what difficulties they have, I could help them on their changing habits journey.

User

I am overweight and would like to change my habits because I want to improve my diet and have a healthier and more balanced life.

Concept

InYou designs and illustrations are based on nature and organic shapes. Every message or illustration is meant to symbolize the journey people need to complete to achieve their goals.

As a wellness-focused company, I incorporated vibrant colors in their design to promote a sense of well-being and community and carefully chose colors that represent each of the three pillars of the app: Exercises (red), Meditation (blue), and Nutrition (green).

App Flow
& Structure

Mapping out the structure and flows of the service gave me a better understanding of how a user will use the app without directly designing the screens.

App Screens

Defining habits

Once registered, users fill out a lifestyle questionnaire. Based on their answers and DNA analysis, we create a personalized program just for them. The questionnaire covers three main areas: meditation, nutrition, and exercise. By understanding their daily routines, we help transform their lives.

Dashboard

After answering the questions, we show a dashboard to users. Here's where they can visualize their habits and mark completed tasks of the day.

Progress Bar

Organizing the day into periods helps users manage their routine and reduces the visual clutter on the dashboard avoiding overwhelming them when entering the app.

Completed tasks

When users finish all the tasks assigned for a specific period, the progress bar shows as complete and the amount of Will points increases. They also receive a congratulatory message, and depending on how frequently they achieve this, they might win a new badge.

Streak Tracker

Users can look at the tracker to monitor their progress and visualize completed and incomplete daily tasks.

Profile & Settings

In this section, it is possible to visualize the test result of the InYou program. The user can also view their achievements and change privacy information in Settings.

Gamification

Will points is a scoring system designed to keep users engaged in their mission

How?

To keep users motivated, we added a gamification system. Users earn points for each day completed and unlock badges as they build new habits.

In the future, users can use their Will points for discounts on healthy products and partners, adding value both inside and outside of the app.
